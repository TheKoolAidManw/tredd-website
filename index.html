What we do

Recycle. Restore.
Redistribute.

Every unused laptop has the potential to change a student's future. We make that happen.

♻️
Collect
We accept unused and unwanted computers from donors across our community.
🛠
Refurbish
Each device is cleaned, repaired, and loaded with educational software by our team.
🌱
Distribute
Computers go directly to children in under-resourced communities — no red tape.
